# Presentation preparation and self-check

These are questions to answer, not a completed 45-minute script.

1. Explain a `(state, action, reward, next_state, done)` transition without looking at the code.
2. Calculate a Q update by hand and explain why terminal states do not bootstrap further.
3. Why might reward differ from the intended objective?
4. What do tabular and neural-network Q-learning represent?
5. What are the respective roles of the actor and critic?
6. Which data or history form the state, and which information is unavailable at decision time?
7. Present an experimental failure or limitation, not only the best run.
8. What changes in the small experiment, and how are other factors held constant?
9. Which conclusions come from experiments, which follow from enforced environment rules, and which remain unproved?
10. How can someone reproduce the same results from scratch?
