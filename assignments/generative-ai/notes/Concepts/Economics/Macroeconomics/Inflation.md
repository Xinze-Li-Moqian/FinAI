# Inflation

Inflation is an increase in a broad price level over a specified period. A price index summarizes price changes across a defined basket or population; one unusually expensive item does not establish the overall inflation rate. The CPI measures consumer price changes for its stated coverage. [BLS: CPI concepts](https://www.bls.gov/opub/hom/cpi/concepts.htm)

For a price index $P$, period inflation is $P_t/P_{t-1}-1$. Specify the index and horizon: monthly and twelve-month changes are different measurements.

**Worked example, calculated here:** an index moving from 100 to 105 implies 5% inflation. If it then reaches 107.1, inflation is 2%; the price level is still rising. Falling positive inflation therefore does not mean prices have returned to their earlier level.
