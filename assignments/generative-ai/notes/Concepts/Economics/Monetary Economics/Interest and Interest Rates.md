# Interest and Interest Rates

**Interest** is the agreed charge a borrower pays for using borrowed money. An **interest rate** expresses that charge as a proportion of the principal over a stated period.

For example, assume a $1,000 loan at 5% per year for one year, repaid at the end, with simple interest and no fees. Interest is **$1,000 × 5% = $50**, so the total repayment is **$1,050**.

Always identify the period, compounding convention and instrument. A loan rate, a bond's fixed coupon rate and its market yield answer different questions. A quoted percentage without those details is incomplete.
