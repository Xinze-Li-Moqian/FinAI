# Monetary Policy

Monetary policy consists of central-bank decisions intended to influence monetary and financial conditions in pursuit of its mandate. The Federal Reserve's US objectives include maximum employment and stable prices. [Federal Reserve: Monetary policy goals and implementation](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/monetary-policy-what-are-its-goals-how-does-it-work.htm)

In the US, the federal funds target range is a major policy instrument; balance-sheet policies and communication also matter. A policy announcement changes the outlook faced by households, businesses and markets. [Federal Reserve: How we conduct monetary policy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/fedexplained/monetary-policy.htm)

When interpreting a statement, distinguish **the objective**, **the instrument**, and **the expected effect**. A rate increase is an action; a particular future inflation outcome is a separate claim requiring evidence.
