# Policy Interest Rates

Policy interest rates are central-bank benchmarks or administered rates used to implement monetary policy. In the US, distinguish the FOMC's federal funds **target range** from the **effective market rate** and from the administered rates used to help implement the target. [Federal Reserve: Monetary policy goals and implementation](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/monetary-policy-what-are-its-goals-how-does-it-work.htm)

**Worked example, hypothetical:** moving a target range from 4.00–4.25% to 4.25–4.50% is a 0.25 percentage-point increase, or 25 basis points. It does not mean every mortgage or ten-year bond yield rises by exactly 25 basis points.
