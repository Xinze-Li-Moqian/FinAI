# Investing During Wars - Cash Out or Buy the Dip?

Immediately following the outbreak of war, it is reasonable to find yourself gripped by fear, asking yourself questions like, "How long will this last? Will anybody that I know be harmed or killed?" Or even, "Is this the end of civilization as we know it?" But I've also found, at least in the US, we also find ourselves asking questions that we may be embarrassed to voice out loud.

Things like, "Is this new war going to make my gas bills or my grocery bills go up? Are we going to get hit by a [[Finance/11 Employment and the Real Economy/Recessions|recession]] because of this potentially putting my job at risk? Or is this going to cause the stock market to crash and wreck my portfolio? Now, some people might say that these questions are unimportant or even morally objectionable at a time like this.

After all, people are dying. Lives are literally being destroyed. There are certainly more honorable things to be worried about right now. And while that all may be true, it doesn't change the fact that you are in fact still worried about these things.
