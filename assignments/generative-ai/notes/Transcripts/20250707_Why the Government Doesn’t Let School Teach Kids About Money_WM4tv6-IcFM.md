The Prussians were the first ones in modern history to adopt a governmentrun school system that was mandatory for all children to attend.

This was back in the late 1700s. And the Prussians used this in order to create a society of soldiers. They wanted to train and brainwash their youth into being a military society. In order to do this, they designed specifically how the public schools would operate. They implemented things like uniforms, bells and buzzers to signal when kids needed to move from one place to the next, walking in line, standardized testing, and school buildings that looked and felt like military barracks.

These are all things that still exist to this day in most modern public schools. And they were originally designed to train and brainwash kids into feeling comfortable with and viewing that military lifestyle as normal. Now, a few people, including the Rockefellers, saw this system and wanted to bring it back to the United States in the 1800s, but with a twist. Instead of having it produce military people, they wanted it to produce factory workers.

And so, over the course of a few decades, public schooling was rolled out across the United States with the express purpose of pumping out factory workers for the new industrial age. These included things like emphasizing standardized testing over original and critical thinking, emphasizing things like obedience over problem solving and ultimately emphasizing certifications like degrees over competence.
