Obviously, you could steal from somebody, but putting that aside, there are only two ways to get money. One is you give somebody else something that they want badly enough that they're willing to give you some of their money for that thing. Now, that could be your time. That could be your labor.

That could be a service. That could be a product. But in order to get money, you've got to come up with something that somebody else wants badly enough to pay you for it. This naturally put a limitation on how fast the money supply could grow because it could only grow if the demand for money was so high that it was affordable for people to spend the money to go get the gold, produce it, and put it into circulation.
