The second perception that this caused was people assuming that diamonds were virtually indestructible, especially given where they sit on the Moe's hardness scale. In fact, the MO scale of mineral hardness is literally rated 1 through 10 with diamond being rated 10. However, that is a scale of hardness referring to how difficult it is to scratch something. Only something harder can scratch something softer.

It has nothing to do with something being indestructible, unbreakable, or impervious to damage. In fact, the burning point of a diamond is only 900° C. That's equivalent to around 1,650° F.
