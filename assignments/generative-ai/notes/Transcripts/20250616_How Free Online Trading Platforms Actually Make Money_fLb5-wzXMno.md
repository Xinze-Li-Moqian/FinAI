Market makers make their money by making sure that there's always liquidity on that security.

And so they want to make money on the spread between the bid and the ask. So they'll get a buyer from somewhere, they'll get a seller from somewhere, they'll match them up, and because they're the middleman, they'll take the spread between the bid and the ask, whether that's 1 cents, 5 cents, or one anything. And so they will go to places like Schwab and Fidelity and Robin Hood and Weeble and Interactive Brokers and they'll say, "Hey, we want you to send us all of your order flow so that we can get the most trades so that we make the most money on this spread.
