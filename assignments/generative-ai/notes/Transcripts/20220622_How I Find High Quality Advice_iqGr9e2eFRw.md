so the pursuit of knowledge wisdom advice direction the pursuit itself is oftentimes just as valuable as the destination which is receiving the advice or the knowledge or the tip that you are looking for and so just start looking it's a numbers game there's a lot of crap out there like i said the problem today is not the lack of available information about something and so you have to start looking the imagery that i like to use for this is being dropped in the middle of the rain forest with a machete and your goal is to get out of the rain forest well you're going to have to start moving in one direction you're going to

have to start slashing down the branches and the vines and the trees and you're going to have to start going in a direction you're going to just have to make a choice to move somewhere you don't know if that's the right direction in in fact in all likelihood it is the wrong direction but the only way you're going to know that is to move forward in that direction so start reading books start watching videos start listening to podcasts start asking people just look start that pursuit because that pursuit itself will help guide you in the direction you're gonna be able to course correct as you go

so you're going to start looking you're going to start looking you're going to find something you'll be like oh that's what i've been looking for and it's going to say turn right so you're going to turn right and then you're going to get a little bit further along and you're going to be like nope that wasn't it but you'll find something that says turn left and so you'll have this zigzag approach while you're chopping your way through the forest through the jungle and eventually you're going to run into something and you're going to say shoot i was trying to find a river or a road or a way out and i just ran into a cliff that goes straight up that means i've wasted all this time

so that's number one start looking it's a numbers game you'll eventually run across the right advice number two disregard anything from somebody who is not already in the same spot that you want to be now this doesn't mean in every area of life but at least in the area that you are trying to progress in always disregard in fact don't even ask unless that person is already where you want to be and so many times people are going to tell you to do something many times people are going to give you advice recommendations tell you their opinion but if they are not currently where you want to be disregard it

because somebody has the relationships and the family life that you want to achieve doesn't mean that the advice they're going to give you is appropriate or will work for you not at all but when you look at the signal to noise ratio on how much signal there is compared to the noise how much good advice compared to bad advice it's going to lean more heavily in your favor if you are seeking that advice from somebody who's already where you want to be instead of somebody who has no idea what it takes to get there

but you can't look too far ahead and that brings us to tip number three which is you can't look too far ahead you can only look about two to three steps ahead of where you're at right now and so again this is the third tip that i use because when i started this process one of the big pieces of this for me is reading books because i don't have a massive network where there are a lot of people who i can just go to i don't know that many people who are where i want to go or at least i didn't used to and so
