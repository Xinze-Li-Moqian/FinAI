The Fed uses the IRB to pay interest on reserves at the Fed in order to make sure that the federal funds effective rate stays in between the federal funds range that it wants it to stay in. Which means that those financial institutions that sold those assets to the Fed now no longer have an asset, but they do have cash that they have to go out there and do something with.

During times of increased government borrowing and spending, this feedback loop can push [[Concepts/Economics/Monetary Economics/Interest and Interest Rates|interest rates]] very close to negative territory.
