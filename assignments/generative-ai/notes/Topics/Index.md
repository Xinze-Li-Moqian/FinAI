## Organization rules

- Store each concept once in `Concepts/<domain>/<topic>/<concept>.md`.
- Choose its primary home by meaning, not by the video in which it appeared.
- Use full vault-relative wikilinks; additional topic pages may reference the same concept.
- Keep navigation pages in `Topics/`, tagged `node/theme`, and concept pages tagged `node/concept`.
- Preserve aliases when moving or renaming a concept.
- Add a topic folder when it has actual concepts; do not create placeholder concepts to balance counts.
- Most existing finance concept pages still contain aliases only. A folder assignment does not imply a completed definition.
