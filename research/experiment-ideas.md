# Experiments

An initial [tabular Q-learning warm-up](../assignments/presentations/01-case44-candidate/warmup/RESULTS.md) has been run with fixed seeds, baseline comparisons and recorded outputs. It is a toy Catch environment, not a reproduction of the financial case.

A possible first study is a simulated fixed-budget allocator over a small set of verification tasks. Compare a simple baseline with one alternative policy under the same budget. Measure completed validated tasks, cost, failed attempts, and duplicated work. Check budget and settlement invariants separately.

Each experiment should record its question, assumptions, data provenance, baseline, metrics, environment, seed where applicable, results, and limitations. Use synthetic or explicitly shareable data; keep credentials and private datasets outside Git.
