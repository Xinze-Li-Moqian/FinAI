on the evening before Monday December
3rd investors in Gold were given what
seemed to be a gift that they had been
waiting years for within a matter of
mere minutes the price of gold began
ripping higher peing at over
$2,144 per ounce and given the fact that
this was taking place directly after
November in which the price of gold saw
the highest monthly close on record it
seemed as if a new bull market had just
been born but unfortunately as many gold
bulls feared this excitement was
shortlived because as quickly as the
price had skyrocketed it fell right back
down to where it was before it seemed as
if the price of gold was getting
hammered back down by some invisible
hands that just control the price of
gold a move that would extinguish every
last drop of hope that precious metals
investors have been hanging on to by a
thread for years
