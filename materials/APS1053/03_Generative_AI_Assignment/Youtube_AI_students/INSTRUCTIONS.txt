Install:

This many transcripts will take you a about 3 or 4 nights.

We will use the downloaded video transcripts in the next steps that will cover:

How to instruct preferably the free Claude LLM (=a Large Language Model) or the free ChatGPT
to program for you a Python workflow that uses another LLM (=DeepSeek)
to construct a knowledge base (=a special file called a mark down file) that
uses an epistemic hierarchy (Claim->Theme->Argument->Evidence & EvolutionOverTime) to
synthesize the many hundreds of transcripts of the chosen crypto finance youtube channel.

The point of creating this knowledge base is:
given the knowledge base, 
any LLM can provide detailed answers 
about the contents of the chosen crypto finance youtube channel without hallucinating.

You will NOT have to program a single line of Python.
We will teach you how to write the prompt for Claude, and how to write prompts in general.
