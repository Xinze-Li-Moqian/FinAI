### Local Theme: Government as a net drain on wealth
Local Theme ID: `LT-BDAD84144ED5`

#### Local Argument: All government jobs are a net drain on wealth because they are funded by involuntary taxation from the productive private sector and do not produce voluntarily demanded goods/services.
Local Argument ID: `LA-E0AEA82DE2AA`

**Premises:**
- Private sector operates through voluntary exchange, producing wealth.
- Government takes money by force (taxation) and uses it to pay workers.
- Government-funded activities often are those the private sector deemed unprofitable.
- Even necessary government functions are still a net transfer, not wealth creation.

**Analogies & Rhetorical Devices:**
